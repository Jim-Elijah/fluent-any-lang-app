## 📱 Cross-Platform Flutter App: `fluent-any-lang-app`

We are bringing the FluentAnyLang experience to mobile and desktop via our official cross-platform application: **FluentAnyLang App**!

* 🌐 **Official Website**: [FluentAnyLang App Website](https://app.fal.jimelijah.com/)
* 📲 **Platforms**: iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux

### Why the Native Flutter App?
While the Web version delivers a zero-install, lightweight practice playground, the native Flutter client addresses critical audio workflow needs:
* 🎧 **Background & Lock-Screen Playback**: Practice shadowing on your daily commute with screen locked and hardware headphone controls.
* 🎙️ **Low-Latency Dual-Track Recording**: Millisecond-accurate voice capture with real-time waveform contrast (Native speaker vs. Your voice).
* 📁 **Seamless Local File System Access**: Drag-and-drop gigabyte-level audio/video libraries without browser sandbox storage quotas.
* ☁️ **BYOS Multi-Device Sync**: Bring Your Own Storage (WebDAV / iCloud / Google Drive) to keep your shadowing progress synchronized across mobile and desktop.

👉 **Visit the [FluentAnyLang App Website](https://app.fal.jimelijah.com/)** to explore feature previews and join the Early Access Beta!

## ✨ Key Features (Web & App)

- 🎧 **Sentence-Level Precision Looping**: Click any sentence in the timeline to loop, slow down, or dissect phonetics.
- 📊 **Dual-Track Waveform Comparison**: Visually inspect your pitch contour and pronunciation cadence against the original audio.
- 🔒 **Local-First & 100% Private**: Your media, recordings, and learning histories stay on your device — no external server uploads.
- 📑 **Universal Format Compatibility**: Smoothly handles common media (MP3, WAV, MP4, MKV) and subtitles (SRT, VTT, LRC).
- 🌍 **Multilingual Interface**: Built-in support for global language learners.
